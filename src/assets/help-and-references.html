Bhagavad Gita Help & References

Read the Bhagavad Gita chapter by chapter, listen to recitations, or study the Sanskrit with Sandhi Vigraha and Anvaya.

Read a chapter

  1. Choose a chapter from the list on the left. On a phone, use the menu button at the start of the toolbar to open the chapter list.
  2. Use the Reading language dropdown to choose English, संस्कृतम् (Sanskrit), or सन्धिविग्रह (Sanskrit Sandhi).
  3. In English or Sanskrit, select a verse to see its meaning and play its audio.

Study Sandhi and Anvaya

Choose सन्धिविग्रह for chapters 1–18. The reader shows the original Sanskrit, grouped verses where applicable, and the chapter’s opening and closing passages when available.

Select a verse or passage to expand it. Sandhi Vigraha shows how joined words separate, and Anvaya puts the verse into prose order. Opening another row collapses the previous one.

Search, downloads, and text size

  • Use Search (the magnifying glass) to find verses in English or Sanskrit.
  • Use the music note to download the chapter recitation. Use the document icon for the chapter PDF in English or Sanskrit, according to your reading language. Select த for the Tamil PDF.
  • Use the small and large A buttons to change the reading text size.

Your browser remembers your chapter and English or Sanskrit preference. When you reopen the reader after using Sandhi, it opens in regular Sanskrit.
